Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-12 · app package com.omnp.alphafeed · contact cauefeder@gmail.com

AlphaFeed (“the app”, “we”) is an informational sports-analytics app. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and your choices. AlphaFeed does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal profile.

AlphaFeed is informational analytics only. It does not accept, place, or facilitate any wager or payment to a bookmaker, and it is not betting or financial advice. For adults 18+.

Information we handle

We do NOT collect your name, email, phone, precise location, contacts, photos, or any account credentials. There is no login.

Advertising (free tier only). The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, which may access your device’s advertising ID and standard ad-request signals (device type, general region) to serve and measure ads. We show a consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads; you can choose non-personalized ads where required by law. See Google’s ads policy. The Pro version shows no ads.

Purchases. If you subscribe to Pro, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing. We receive only your entitlement status (whether an active subscription exists) — never your card or payment details, which Google processes.

Network requests. The app fetches analytics from our backend (alphafeed-api.onrender.com) over HTTPS. These requests carry only standard technical metadata and no personal data about you. We do not build a profile of you.

On-device data. The app caches the latest analytics and your settings locally on your device so it works offline. This never leaves your device.

How the data is used

Solely to (a) show and measure ads on the free tier, (b) unlock Pro features for subscribers, and (c) deliver the analytics content. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

We share data only with the providers that make the app work — Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Play services) under their terms. No one else.

Your choices

  • Ads: reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; adjust consent in the app’s prompt.
  • Cached data: clear it via Settings → Apps → AlphaFeed → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel in the Google Play Store → Subscriptions.

Children

AlphaFeed concerns betting-market analytics and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. The app stores no personal data and has no account.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Effective” date above will change and material changes ship in an app update.
