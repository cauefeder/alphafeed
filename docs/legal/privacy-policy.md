# AlphaFeed — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-12
**App:** AlphaFeed (package `com.omnp.alphafeed`)
**Contact:** cauefeder@gmail.com

AlphaFeed ("the app", "we") is an **informational sports-analytics** app. This policy explains what
data the app accesses, why, and your choices. **AlphaFeed does not require an account and does not
ask for your name, email, or any personal profile.**

## What the app is (and isn't)
AlphaFeed shows model-generated analytics about publicly traded prediction/sports markets
(expected value, estimated win probability, price). It is **informational only** — it does **not**
accept, place, or facilitate any wager or payment to a bookmaker, and it is **not** betting or
financial advice.

## Information we handle

**We do NOT collect:** your name, email, phone, precise location, contacts, photos, or any account
credentials. There is no login.

**Advertising (free tier only).** The free version shows ads via **Google AdMob**. AdMob may access
your device's **advertising ID** and standard ad-request data (approximate, coarse signals such as
device type and general region) to serve and measure ads. Before serving personalized ads we show a
consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform); you can choose non-personalized ads where
required by law. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The **Pro**
(paid) version shows **no ads**.

**Purchases.** If you subscribe to Pro, the transaction is handled by **Google Play Billing**. We
receive only your **entitlement status** (whether an active subscription exists) — never your card
or payment details, which Google processes. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Network requests.** The app fetches analytics from our backend (`alphafeed-api.onrender.com`).
These requests carry only standard technical metadata (e.g., IP address at the network level, needed
to deliver the response) and **no personal data about you**. We do not build a profile of you.

**On-device data.** The app caches the latest analytics and your settings (theme, consent choice,
onboarding-seen) **locally on your device** so it works offline. This never leaves your device.

## How the data is used
Solely to (a) show and measure ads on the free tier, (b) unlock Pro features for subscribers, and
(c) deliver the analytics content. We do **not** sell your data.

## Sharing
We share data only with the service providers that make the app work: **Google** (AdMob, Play
Billing, Play services) under their terms. We do not share data with anyone else.

## Your choices
- **Ads:** reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; adjust consent in
  the app's ad-consent prompt.
- **Cached data:** clear it any time via Android Settings → Apps → AlphaFeed → Storage → Clear data,
  or by uninstalling.
- **Subscription:** manage or cancel in the Google Play Store → Subscriptions.

## Children
AlphaFeed concerns betting-market analytics and is intended for adults (**18+**). It is not directed
to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## Security
Network traffic uses HTTPS. Because the app stores no personal data and has no account, there is no
personal profile to breach.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Effective date" above will change. Material changes will be reflected
in an app update.
